# Pokémon 1.4.0 — Aperture

Aperture replaces Trainer Archive under the existing saved dark-theme preference. It has its own header and navigation markup, a desktop field/command-station layout, a party roster, and a mobile stack. Journey, party, Pokédex, services and settings use the same visual system. Choose Aperture from Pokémon settings; Original is still available.

Captures hide only the in-field HP plates for the full animation. The command panel stays visible; its controls are disabled until completion. The action dispatcher also rejects attempts during animation, including keyboard or programmatic activation through the interface. Buttons that were disabled before motion stay disabled afterward. Capture balls settle on the opposing terrain base, not the central gap. No title or skip control overlays the arena. Escape can skip a sequence on a keyboard. Reduced motion bypasses animation.

The presentation follows saved game outcomes. Browser checks use a disposable SillyTavern installation, including desktop and phone emulation; no live model or physical iPhone is used. The showcase recordings have no audio track.
